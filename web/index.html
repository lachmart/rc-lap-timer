    Drivers in turn

    Tap to leave a driver out, tap again to put them at the end. After a driver's last lap the timer hands the car to the next one; their time starts at the car's next crossing. Fastest total wins.

    Starting a race clears the session. A countdown runs (3, 2, 1, go); each car's first crossing after "go" starts its timing. The race ends when the leader completes the last lap (or crosses after the time is up); every other car finishes at its next crossing.

      Wi-Fi

        Join your home Wi-Fi so the phone can stay on it with internet; the timer is then at laptimer.local. The RC_Lap_Timer network always runs too, and changes here don't restart the timer. Out of range, the timer retries every 30 s while no phone is on RC_Lap_Timer. At the track, a phone hotspot works as the home network.

        USB / ZRound

        Connect the timer to the laptop with ZRound by USB and choose the RiCino decoder, 19200 baud. ZRound then runs the races itself; this app keeps showing the passes. Only transponder cars are sent. While this is on, the USB port has no debug log.
